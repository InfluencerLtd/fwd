# The mark, as it arrived

`lockup-source.svg` and `lockup-source.png` are the artwork that was approved
early on, kept here unchanged.

**Nothing on the site uses it any more.** The arrow glyph in that artwork was
traced into real outlines and put on the hero, both header bars, the footer, the
browser tab and the share image — and then taken off all of them on the brief
that fwd. should be a word mark rather than an icon. An icon reads as a brand of
its own, and this one has to sit beside influencer and beside partner brands
without competing with either.

What the site uses now:

| Where | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| The lockup | "fwd." set in Instrument Serif, with "by influencer" under or beside it. There is no separate mark |
| `src/app/icon.svg`, `favicon.ico`, `apple-icon.png` | "f." from the same face, on the header's near-black |

The tab icon is the one place a word mark cannot go: "fwd." at sixteen pixels is
an unreadable smudge, which is why brands with word marks still ship something
square. "f." is a letterform out of the word rather than a new symbol, and it is
still legible at thirty-two.

These files sit outside `public/` on purpose: nothing here is served.
